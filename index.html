A barbershop that respects your time and your head

BLEND began with a simple rule: every cut is booked long enough to be done properly.

We pair traditional barbering, hot towels, straight razors and patient scissor work, with a calm, modern room. No loud queues, no assembly-line fades.

Every appointment starts with a short consultation about your hair type, your routine and how you want to look on Monday morning. Then we get to work.
